Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Our standard check-in time begins at 3:00 PM, allowing our team sufficient time to prepare your room and ensure everything is ready for a comfortable arrival. Check-out is requested by 11:00 AM on your departure day. If you plan to arrive earlier or would prefer to leave later, please contact our reception team in advance. Early check-in and late check-out requests are subject to room availability and may involve additional charges. We recommend sharing your expected arrival time when making your reservation so we can make your arrival as smooth and convenient as possible.

Are pets allowed inside the chalets?

We understand that pets are an important part of your family and that travelling is often more enjoyable when they can join you. Selected chalets may accommodate pets, subject to our pet policy and availability. If you are planning to bring your furry companion, please inform our team when making your reservation so we can confirm the appropriate accommodation and explain any applicable charges or restrictions. We also recommend checking our guidelines regarding pet supervision, designated areas and shared facilities before your arrival. Our goal is to help you plan a comfortable stay for every member of your family.

How do I book private dining or helicopter tours?

Our concierge team can help you arrange personalised experiences during your stay, including private dining, special celebrations and selected sightseeing activities. To make arrangements, contact us through WhatsApp or speak with our team before your arrival. We recommend booking special experiences in advance, particularly during weekends, holidays and busy travel periods. Availability, pricing and weather conditions may affect certain activities, including helicopter tours. Our team will explain the available options, help coordinate the arrangements and share any important requirements so you can plan your experience with confidence.

What room types and accommodation options are available?

We offer a selection of accommodation options designed to suit different travel preferences, whether you are planning a romantic getaway, a family holiday or a relaxing break with friends. Depending on availability, guests can explore options such as private chalets, spacious suites and family-friendly accommodation. Each room category may offer different layouts, views, amenities and occupancy limits. We recommend reviewing our Rooms and Amenities page before booking to compare the available options and select the accommodation that best suits your requirements. If you need assistance choosing a room, our team can help you identify a suitable option for your planned stay.

What amenities and facilities can guests enjoy during their stay?

We aim to make every stay relaxing and convenient by offering thoughtfully selected amenities and guest services. Depending on your accommodation category, facilities may include comfortable bedding, private seating areas, complimentary toiletries, Wi-Fi access and scenic outdoor spaces. Additional services and experiences may be available upon request. As facilities can vary between room categories, we encourage you to review the amenities listed for your preferred accommodation before confirming your reservation. If you have specific requirements, such as accessibility needs or particular in-room facilities, please contact our team beforehand so we can clarify what is available for your stay.

Is advance booking necessary, especially during holidays?

Advance booking is recommended to help you secure your preferred accommodation and travel dates. Availability can become limited during weekends, public holidays, festive periods and popular travel seasons. Booking early also gives you more time to review room categories, understand the available facilities and plan any additional experiences you would like to enjoy. Before completing your reservation, please double-check your check-in date, check-out date, number of guests and selected room type. If your preferred dates are unavailable, you can contact our team to enquire about alternative dates or other suitable accommodation options.
