Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in begins at 3:00 PM, and check-out is requested by 11:00 AM. Early arrivals or late departures can be requested based on availability.

Are pets allowed inside the chalets?

Yes! Selected chalets at Hotel bhopal are pet-friendly. Please mention your furry companion during booking so we can arrange special amenities for them.

How do I book private dining or helicopter tours?

You can easily coordinate these experiences with our concierge desk via WhatsApp or directly upon checking in at the resort.
